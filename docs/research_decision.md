# Research decision

**GO for a reproducible technical study; NO-NEW-METHOD claim.** Julia is the
canonical implementation, with frozen Python evidence and a limited independent
R reference. The scientific milestone is a transparent exact-transition
likelihood core, independently derived formulas and auditable numerical
comparisons. Executed validation and development results are reported in
[findings.md](findings.md).

The explicit nuisance profile, restricted KL calculation and chronological split
coverage argument are useful verification results. They are elementary
specializations of established theory, not a new universal-inference theorem.
Existing literature already addresses diffusion drift bias, persistence,
initial conditions, fixed-span asymptotics and grid-bootstrap inference.
The literature review remains incomplete; unavailable full texts are listed in
[literature_map.md](literature_map.md).

The preserved development pilot found high split-set membership alongside very
little ability to exclude zero. That negative informativeness finding is retained.
It does not establish superiority, full coverage over nuisance parameters,
confidence-set widths or a clean infill comparison. Failure of the uncalibrated
chi-square diagnostic does not establish failure of properly calibrated
likelihood inference.

The next narrowly justified milestone is a faithful grid-bootstrap reproduction
and confidence-set geometry on the full nonnegative parameter space. Identify
the paper version, reproduce its initialization and nuisance handling, and then
label any adaptation required for unrestricted drift or irregular schedules.
The July 2020 working manuscript located during the literature refresh is not
interchangeable with the 2022 journal article. No baseline algorithm is declared
reproduced merely because a paper or abstract was retrieved.

Only after that comparison should a fixed-span study separate intrinsic
information limitations from nuisance estimation and the cost of a chronological
split. Exact nested paths and common prefixes will separate sampling-density
effects from observation-span effects. At present there is no proved new
OU-specific efficiency result, certified continuum endpoint procedure, locked
confirmation study or new-method contribution.

A public artifact may accurately be described as a **reproducible technical
study of exact OU inference with development evidence** once its recorded checks
pass. It should not be described as a paper, accepted publication, validated
bootstrap benchmark or novel inferential procedure.
